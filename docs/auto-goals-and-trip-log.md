# Auto goals and Trip Log

Aggregate goals are stored independently for Day, Week, Money, Month, Year, and Custom, starting at 100%. Auto and the matching aggregate mode share that scope's goal. An Auto lock persists the selected goal identity, recalculating its inferred value as trips change. Trip goal is stored separately.

The Derived Trip Goals pane has three rows under Lock (SVG icon), Goal, and Target columns. The first row uses a native aggregate dropdown; the other rows are Trip and Standard. Only aggregate scopes appear in the dropdown. Trip never changes the selected aggregate. Standard displays a plain, borderless 100% that cannot be edited. Percentages are whole values rounded upward. Week labels use the fiscal-year calendar anchor and civil dates. Brackets around the week number are exclusive to Trip Log Settings (Week [40]); Auto and other labels use Week 40.

Native radios select the exclusive draft override, with no selected radio when no override is active. A single tap on a row or its radio toggles that draft lock, including clearing a selected radio. OK applies the draft and closes the pane; Cancel, Back, Escape, or dismissal discards it. Enter and Space on a radio toggle its draft state; Enter on OK confirms. Percentage buttons edit the staged goal separately from radio/confirmation hit areas. The contrasting header and sticky action footer follow the active theme; OK and Cancel remain visible while scrolling. Choosing another aggregate moves an aggregate lock to that scope while preserving Trip or Standard locks.

The popover reduces vertical padding when needed, stays above the microphone bar, and scrolls when minimum control sizes still require it. Draft refreshes retain scroll. Custom range selection opens Trip Log Settings without applying the draft until confirmation.

Trip Log keeps its aggregate and trip panes independent of Settings and Down Time Details. Live ticks reconcile existing elements, preserving scroll and active-trip animation. Closing Trip Log closes child dialogs. All aggregate/day headers remain visible under presentation filters. A hidden-only aggregate has no disclosure triangle. Finalized hidden counts appear beneath individual day headings, with no range-wide hidden summary.

Day ranges retain the totals, Day aggregate header, and adjustable splitter above the trip pane, including historical Day ranges. A one-day range is still an aggregate; it must not hide the upper pane. Empty ranges keep the existing centered empty-state message.

Live trip updates must not rewrite unchanged settings selections or option labels. In particular, replacing identical Week text refreshes Android's open native picker and causes flickering. Update the Week label only when its text changes, so historical range changes and Return to Active still refresh the fiscal week number without disturbing an open selector on every tick.

Selecting a Down Time category chooses the closest combination of existing checks and pending-mode states that includes all contributing trips. Extra trips may appear; no invisible category filter is added. If checks change, Down Time Details closes, Settings pulses changed checks, then closes. Otherwise it returns directly to Trip Log. Saved preferences remain distinct from temporary effective overrides.

Regression coverage includes independent goal persistence, staged confirmation/cancel, radio clearing, bar gestures, custom ranges, fiscal weeks, both themes at phone sizes, live pane identity/scroll, category/check combinations, and gray accent contrast. Browser tests do not establish performance on a physical phone.

Drop-In uses the same three-row Auto popover and exact Goal Bar hit regions as Order Filler. Its aggregate goals, selected aggregate, and exclusive lock identity belong to the observer's Default/selected-user preferences. Tap a bar goal to edit its percentage; hold it in Auto to lock or clear it, choosing among merged identities when necessary. Standard can be locked but its publisher-owned duration remains read-only. The header lock and bar pins follow the observer's effective configuration. Mirror restores the publisher's configuration.

Auto percentage edits remain staged until OK; Cancel/dismissal discards them. Drop-In then uses its existing Default/User targeting dialog to choose where to save. Aggregate goals also apply when switching from Auto to that aggregate's ordinary view. Selecting a different aggregate loads that user's authorized range totals, excluding the active trip before replay adds it exactly once. Custom range dates are staged in a date dialog. Switching observed users discards an unfinished Auto draft and numberpad session. No observer control writes publisher trip data.

Auto ignores the saved Sync setting. Its Trip candidate uses the independent configured Trip goal, its aggregate candidate uses that aggregate’s derived goal, and Standard remains 100%; Goal Bounding applies independently. Keeping Sync enabled must not change Auto nodes, locks, countdowns, or time/percent readouts. Preserve the preference and resume Sync on returning to Trip. Apply the same rule to observer replay and reload.

Enabling Sync from the hamburger or main button opens the centered Sync Goals aggregate chooser. Day is the default when no range is saved; the existing aggregate is selected and focused. Only aggregate buttons are shown, with no X or OK/Cancel footer. Escape, backdrop clicks and generic Close cannot dismiss it. Choosing any aggregate, including the selected one, loads that range and enables Sync. Turning Sync off remains direct. Voice Sync on/off remains direct and never opens this chooser.

Custom expands Start/End fields in the same chooser. The first Custom tap reveals the draft dates; tapping Custom again validates and applies them. If Custom is already the selected range, the saved dates are visible immediately and one tap retains them. Invalid dates leave the chooser open and Sync unchanged. Switching to another aggregate discards the date draft. There are no extra action buttons.
