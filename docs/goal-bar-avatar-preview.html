Walking

Break → Down stands while the chair exits, then raises the arm. Down → Break lowers the arm, brings the chair in and sits. Restoring an active state plays its entrance only. Finish confetti runs once; idle snapshots do not replay it. Reduced-motion preferences suppress travel and confetti.
